# Sync licence request: Tekno, "Rara"

## Who to send it to

A song has two sets of rights, and an ad needs **both**:

| Right | Covers | Who usually owns it | Where to find them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Master** (the recording) | The actual audio of Tekno's "Rara" | The record label | The ℗ line under the song on Spotify / Apple Music ("℗ 20XX [label name]") |
| **Publishing** (the composition) | The melody and lyrics | The music publisher(s) and songwriter(s) | The song's credits on Apple Music / Spotify; or ask the label who the publisher is |

The fastest first contact is usually **Tekno's management or booking email** (often in his official Instagram bio). Ask them to forward the request, or to name the label and publisher contacts.

Don't use the song in any public post until a signed licence and the invoice are in hand.

## Email (copy, fill the [brackets], send)

**Subject:** Sync licence request: "Rara" by Tekno, 30-second social media ad (GroSolar)

Hello [Name],

I'm writing from GroSolar Energy, a Lagos-based solar financing company. We have produced a 30-second vertical video ad, and we would love to use Tekno's song **"Rara"** as its music.

We are requesting a **synchronisation licence** for both the **master recording** and the **composition**, on these terms:

- **Use:** the ad opens with 6 seconds of the song (0:29–0:35, the NEPA/generator lyric), then continues with **the instrumental version of "Rara"** under our voiceover for the remaining 24 seconds. About 30 seconds in total, edited for length and faded at the end. No changes to lyrics or melody, and no remixing.
- **Media:** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and WhatsApp (organic posts and paid promotion), plus GroSolar's own website.
- **Territory:** Nigeria.
- **Term:** 12 months from first publication, with an option to renew.
- **Credit:** "Music: 'Rara' by Tekno" in the post caption, or as you prefer.

Could you please let us know:
1. Whether this use is possible, and who handles the master and publishing rights.
2. The fee and any conditions.
3. How we would receive high-quality audio files (WAV or 320 kbps MP3) once the licence is agreed: the full song **and the instrumental** (or the stems, if that is easier).

We can share the video in advance for approval. Thank you, and we look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,
[Your name]

## After a yes

Send the licence (or a confirmation email) and the audio file. The ad will be re-mixed with "Rara" under the voiceover, at the same quality checks, in the same session. Until then, the ad ships with a free, commercially licensed track (see `AUDIO-SOURCING.md`).
