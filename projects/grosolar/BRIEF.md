## 3. The core message (working, not final)

- The problem every viewer already feels: unreliable grid power and the cost and noise of generator fuel.
- The barrier: solar is expensive up front.
- GroSolar's answer: **we fund it, you get solar now, you pay over time.**
- The proof: real installations and real numbers (see section 5).
- The action: call or WhatsApp.

How each audience hears it in one line (the brand ad touches each briefly):

| Audience | What they care about |
|---|---|
| Homeowners | Steady power at home, no generator noise or fuel runs, an affordable payment |
| Small businesses | Stay open during outages, lower running costs, predictable monthly payment |
| Large commercial | Cut diesel spend at scale, no capital outlay, reliability |
| Partners / investors | A growing, funded pipeline of real installations |

## 4. Truth rules (from the kit's non-negotiables)

The ad may only say things GroSolar can back up. **Never** claim without proof: savings percentages, "free" solar, zero interest, approval for everyone, warranties, guaranteed uptime, client names or testimonials without their permission.

Payment terms the ad can state (confirmed by owner):
- **Nothing up front.** GroSolar funds the whole system.
- **Pay over time:** amortized payments, spread over a period set by what the client can afford.
- **Who it is for:** anyone interested with good credit history / healthy bank statements. Say "subject to approval" or similar; never "everyone qualifies".
- **GroSolar's team installs and maintains** the system.
- **The client owns the system** once it is paid off.
