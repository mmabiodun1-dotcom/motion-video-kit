# GroSolar ad: voiceover recording guide

Who: one woman's voice (owner's choice: a real person, recorded on a phone).
Tone: warm, confident, unhurried; like telling a friend good news, not shouting an advert. Natural Nigerian English.

## The six lines

Record each line **three times** in a row, with a 2-second pause between takes. One file for everything is fine.

| # | Line | Where it plays | Time it must fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Less generator. More sun. | The sunlight sweeps the shop | about 2.5 s |
| 2 | GroSolar funds your solar, for homes, businesses and industry, with nothing upfront. | Panels → home/shop/warehouse → "NOTHING UPFRONT" | about 6.5 s (a short pause after "solar" is good) |
| 3 | Installed and maintained for you. | Hillside solar farm | about 2.3 s |
| 4 | Pay in instalments. Once it's paid off, it's yours. | The payment bar fills and stamps OWNED | about 4 s |
| 5 | Real GroSolar projects. | Project photos | about 2 s |
| 6 | Call or WhatsApp to check if you qualify. Get the GroSolar NG app. | End card with the phone number | about 5.5 s |

Say "**GroSolar**" as "Grow-Solar", the same way every time.

## How to record on a phone (5 minutes)

1. Use a quiet room with soft things around (a bedroom with curtains and a bed is ideal). Turn off fans, AC and the generator.
2. Use the phone's voice recorder app, not a video app.
3. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side so the "p" sounds don't pop.
4. Stand or sit up straight and smile slightly while speaking. It really does make the voice warmer.
5. Before the first line, record 5 seconds of silence (room tone). It helps clean the recording.
6. Send the file as it comes off the phone (.m4a, .wav or .mp3). Don't apply any effects.

## What happens next

The best take of each line is picked, cleaned (noise removal, levels, a gentle EQ), and placed on the picture so every line lands on its scene. The final mix follows the kit's audio rules: music under the voice, a few soft sound effects, about −14 LUFS for social feeds.
