2. **Kill empty frames.** Overlap the outgoing and incoming scenes by at least 6 frames at 4.30, 6.60, 9.37, 11.40, 14.00 and 18.45. Hand the beam/line over at exact pixel coordinates: rail → underline → bar → card rail.
3. **Fix the collisions.** "Less" enters after "AGAIN?" exits. Small print hides until the photo settles. "Then it's yours." becomes a 72 px headline at y≥1420. Scale OWNED. to x 80–940 and land it above the bar. Start the rail at y≥360.

5. **Snap the counter** to 100 → 200 → 300 → 450 as each card lands. No intermediate values.
6. **End card:** a transparent logo (SVG or alpha PNG), no box. A 3% push on the whole lockup and slow ray rotation instead of the corner smudge. A phone/WhatsApp pill behind the number to fill y 1300–1500. CTA about 5 s.
7. **Trim holds** (2.9–4.2, 7.9–9.3, 12.8–13.9, 15–18.4; about 3.5 s) and give the time to the proof cards.
8. **Fill dead space.** Icons about 520 px wide. Raise "NOTHING UPFRONT." to y≈600, photo below. Use Sinari, not the broadcaster again, at 11.4.
