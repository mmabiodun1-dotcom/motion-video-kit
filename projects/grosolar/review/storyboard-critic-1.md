# Storyboard critic 1: GroSolar 30s vertical (v0.1)

**Verdict: REVISE.** The sunlight line and OWNED stamp work. Chronology is backwards, three beats are over-worded, proof is unconfirmed, CTA is late.

## Problems, most serious first

1. **Ownership is shown before installation (16.4).** Real order: approval → funding → install → payments → ownership. *Fix:* move install footage to 11.4–14.0, after NOTHING UPFRONT; the payment bar then ends on OWNED.

2. **The proof beat (18.4–24.4) claims more than is confirmed.** "Already running" is unconfirmed. Project 4 has no name or location, so a map pin for it invents one. The broadcaster's location is unknown. "450 kW" is only true if project 4 is real. *Fix:* drop project 4 and the map. Use three real site photos as a card stack: "100 kW · Victoria Island", "100 kW · Epe", "150 kW · National broadcaster". Voiceover: "Real GroSolar projects." Show no total until the owner confirms one.

3. **The voiceover is too fast in three beats.** 2.6–4.4 has 8 words in 1.8 s (4.4 w/s). 12.2–16.4 has 16 words in 4.2 s (3.8). 16.4–18.4 has 7 words in 2.0 s (3.5). The 6 s map beat has 4 words but overloaded visuals. *Fix:* use the script below.

4. **Three lines overpromise.**
   - "At a pace that works for you" is wrong: GroSolar sets the term, the client doesn't choose it.
   - "We maintain it" with no end date reads as lifetime maintenance.
   - "Switch off the generator" implies the power never fails.

   *Fix:* "Pay in instalments." / "Our team installs and maintains it." (owner to confirm whether maintenance continues after payoff) / "Less generator. More sun."

5. **No condition on the offer frame (10.0–12.2).** A finance claim and its condition belong on the same frame (FCCPC, Nigerian Code of Advertising Practice). *Fix:* show "Subject to approval. Terms apply." at 44 px or more under NOTHING UPFRONT, and hold it through the payment bar. Budget for ARCON (Advertising Standards Panel) pre-approval; social ads aren't exempt.

6. **The CTA is late and crowded.** The voice says "Call or WhatsApp" at 24.4, but the number only appears at 27.0. The end card has six things to read. On mute, there is no logo until 24 s. *Fix:* merge the logo beat into the CTA:
   - From 23.0 to 29.9: logo, **0705 370 0000** at 110 px or more with WhatsApp and phone icons, and "Check if you qualify".
   - Below that: one small "GroSolar NG app · Google Play" line and the small print.
   - Keep it all inside y 250–1500 and x ≤ 940.
   - Add a small logo bug from 4.4 s.
   - Export at 29.9 s or shorter.

7. **The hook is a cliché and doesn't work on mute.** Generator openers are standard in Nigerian solar ads; locals say "light", not "power"; silence is invisible on mute. *Fix:*
   - Frame 0: a bright shop at the moment the light dies (the fan slows, the owner looks up), with **NO LIGHT. AGAIN?**
   - Cut to the generator at 0.8 s and flip its switch at 1.6 s.
   - Make the silence visible: the vibration stops dead and the haze clears.
   - Keep the generator outdoors.

8. **The code-built pieces risk looking cheap.** 3D home/shop/factory scenes tend to come out as Western suburbia. Locals will spot a wrong Lagos coastline at once. *Fix:* no map. Draw the three scenes flat and isometric in local styles (a duplex with a parapet, a shop with a roller shutter, a warehouse). Component Gauntlet first.

9. **Missing inputs and footage risks.**
   - The unknown logo and colours block 24.4–30 and every colour token.
   - Close-up panel footage that matches the beam's angle probably doesn't exist.
   - Install footage without harnesses or protective gear would be a liability on screen.
   - Site photos that show broadcaster or client branding name them anyway.

   *Fix:* get logo and footage before building; screen clips for safety gear and branding.

10. **Out of date.** Phone still "TO CONFIRM", voice "To decide". *Fix:* phone confirmed; female AI voice, Nigerian English accent, tested on "GroSolar" and "Epe".

11. **Next step unclear.** No service area, no reason to call, no reason for the app; homeowners see only 100 kW+ commercial sites. *Fix:* CTA line "Check if you qualify"; add "Lagos" to the end card once confirmed.
