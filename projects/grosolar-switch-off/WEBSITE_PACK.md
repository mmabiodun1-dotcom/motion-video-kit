## How it works (the site's four steps)

1. **Get started and select your provider.** Fill in your requirements and pick a preferred solar provider for the site visit and proposal.
2. **Site visit and assessment.** The provider needs roof access and your electricity bills. You get a proposal and finalise the financing.
3. **Installation.** Happens after the agreement is signed: inspections, then the provider helps with activation and app setup.
4. **Pay monthly.** Small monthly instalments.

- Installs are done by **partner solar providers** (Arnergy, Solad, Spirano and others), not by GroSolar directly.
- Strategic partner: **Clean Energy Fund (CEF)**, a local-currency clean energy fund providing long-term naira funding.

## Pre-qualification form (web, four steps)

1. **Personal info**
2. **Property:** owner or business; property type; monthly Disco (electricity) bill; monthly diesel/fuel spend; roof space
3. **Energy loads:** TV, bulbs, fan, fridge, freezer, washing machine, pumps, AC, cooker, iron, microwave, kettle
4. **Location**
