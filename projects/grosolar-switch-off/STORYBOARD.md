# GroSolar — "Switch Off" · Storyboard and script (60 s, 9:16 master)

**VO** = narrator (warm, Nigerian English, slips into Pidgin). **Line** = a character speaks. **Super** = on-screen text. Every spoken line is also captioned.
Items marked **[TBC]** depend on GroSolar confirming a fact or supplying an app screen.

## The persistent actor
A **round on/off switch**. It starts on the generator, becomes the app's "Get started" button, and ends as the sun in the end card. It is the one object that survives the whole film.

## Three signature transformations
1. **The generator switch becomes the app button.** The switch the hand turns off in the hook is the exact shape and position of the "Get started" button when the phone rises.
2. **The four worlds become the four choices.** The home, shop, farm and factory from Act 1 shrink into the app's four plan cards. Picking one expands it back into that world, now running on solar.
3. **The fuel receipt becomes the monthly plan.** The crumpled fuel receipt from Act 1 flattens, its lines re-order, and it settles as the monthly plan card.

## Sound
- The hook relies on **roar → silence**. Act 1 is layered generator noise; it drops out at #2, comes back under #3–8, and is gone for good from #10.
- Music enters at the turn (#10): warm Afrobeats-leaning groove, mid-tempo (~100–105 BPM), no vocals, built to resolve on the end card.
- Clean UI sounds only on real taps in Act 3; one soft whoosh per real transition (`business-motion-film/references/audio.md`).
