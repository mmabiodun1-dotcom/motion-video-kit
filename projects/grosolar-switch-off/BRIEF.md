# GroSolar — "Switch Off" · Brief

**Status:** client film, made with GroSolar. Brand and wording come from `WEBSITE_PACK.md`.

## The business
GroSolar Energy (Lagos, Nigeria · grosolar.co) provides solar as a service. Households and businesses subscribe and pay a predictable monthly fee instead of buying a system upfront. Partner solar providers (Arnergy, Solad, Spirano and others) do the site visit, proposal, installation and activation. GroSolar's strategic partner, the Clean Energy Fund (CEF), provides long-term naira funding. After installation, the provider helps the customer set up the GroSolar NG app.

## The buyer
Everyone running a generator. The site names two categories: **households** and **businesses**. Farms/agribusiness and commercial & industrial sites appear in the film as kinds of business **only if GroSolar confirms they serve them**; the site doesn't mention them (see storyboard fallbacks).

## The viewer's problem
Power cuts, then the generator: the noise, the fumes, the fuel queue and the fuel bill that keeps coming. Most people already know solar is the answer. The upfront cost is what stops them.

## The single action (CTA)
**Get started at grosolar.co** (the pre-qualification form). Secondary: WhatsApp **+234 705 370 0000** (to confirm). The GroSolar NG app is shown as part of membership, not as the first step, because customers set it up at activation.

## Concept
**Switch Off.** The generator is the villain. The film opens on its roar and ends with every generator going quiet. GroSolar is the switch: get started online, choose home or business, a partner provider visits, you get a proposal, it's installed, and you pay monthly.

## Format
- 60 s master, **9:16 1080×1920** for Instagram Reels, TikTok and WhatsApp Status (Status caps clips at about 60 s, so the master sits at the limit).
- **16:9 1920×1080** reframe for YouTube.
- Cut-downs from the same material: 30 s and 15 s (see `STORYBOARD.md`).
- Burned-in captions throughout. It must work on mute.
- Safe zones: keep text and UI out of the bottom ~20% and right ~12% of the 9:16 frame (TikTok/Reels UI).

## Language and voice
English, with Pidgin in character lines and a few supers. The narrator speaks English warmly with a Nigerian accent and drops into Pidgin for punchlines. Every spoken line is also on screen.

## Tone
Warm, funny in Act 1 (the pain everyone recognises), calm and confident from the turn onward. This is not a tech-launch film: it is people getting their evenings and their business back.

## Look
- GroSolar navy `#002554`, yellow `#FCD733` → orange `#FF9733` gradient, light `#F5F9FF`. Type in **Outfit**, headlines ExtraBold 800.
- Act 1 is warm-dark and hazy (sodium light, generator smoke). From the turn, everything goes bright, airy and sunlit, with the brand gradient.
- The round switch is drawn in the brand gradient once it turns "on" at the end.

## Never claim
- Any naira price, savings figure, payback period, percentage or "X customers" number unless GroSolar supplies it in writing.
- Warranties, uptime, "24/7 power", installation times or sign-up times.
- Real customers. All people and places are AI-generated or acted and labelled **"Dramatisation"**. Don't use the site's testimonials; they look like template placeholders.
- Partner providers' names or logos on screen without their consent.
- App features the real app doesn't have.
